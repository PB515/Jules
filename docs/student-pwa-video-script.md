# Synergy — Student PWA Explainer Video Script

*A shot-by-shot script for recording on a real phone with the app actually
installed. Record each numbered shot as its own clip (10–40s) — easier to
re-shoot one bad take than the whole thing, and easier to cut together after.
Screen-record with your phone's native recorder (iOS: Control Center →
Screen Recording; Android: Quick Settings → Screen Record). Total runtime
target: ~3–4 minutes.*

**Before recording**: use a real, already-created student account (not a
disposable test one you'll delete) — pick one from the roster, or ask
someone to demo their own phone. Make sure at least one real event exists
with attendance open, so shots 4 and 5 have something real to show.

**For this first event specifically**: it's participation-only (register,
check in, earn points — no quiz). Shot 6 (Live Round) covers a feature
that exists but isn't part of this event, so it's framed as "coming soon"
below, not something students should expect to use right away. Cut it
entirely if the video needs to be shorter and quiz nights aren't imminent.

---

## Shot 1 — Install (0:00–0:25)

**Show:** Open a browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android) to
`jules-delta-nine.vercel.app/get-app` (or scan the QR code shown on that
page). Tap through the install flow — "Add to Home Screen" on iPhone, the
install prompt/banner on Android.

**Say (voiceover or on-camera):**
> "Synergy only works installed as an app on your phone — not in a browser
> tab. Here's how to get it: open this link, and add it to your home
> screen."

**End on:** the new Synergy icon sitting on the home screen.

---

## Shot 2 — First open, login (0:25–0:55)

**Show:** Tap the home-screen icon. The launch splash plays (logo + "Progress,
Together."). Land on the login screen, type in a real student email/password,
tap in.

**Say:**
> "Open it like any other app. Log in with the account your club gave you —
> if you don't have one yet, your club coordinator creates it for you, you
> don't sign up yourself."

**End on:** the Dashboard loading.

---

## Shot 3 — Dashboard tour (0:55–1:35)

**Show:** Slowly scroll the Dashboard top to bottom: Season Points hero
number, tier badge (Ember/Volt/Current/Plasma), the Power Grid, the streak
chain, Upcoming Events, My Registered Events.

**Say:**
> "This is your Grid — your home screen. Your Synergy Points for this season,
> your tier, and your streak — how many events in a row you've actually
> shown up to. Below that: events you can register for, and the ones you're
> already signed up for."

**End on:** tapping one "Upcoming event" row.

---

## Shot 4 — Register for an event (1:35–2:05)

**Show:** The event detail page — name, date/time, venue, points value. Tap
"Register." If the event has an external form linked, show it opening too.
Go back to the Dashboard, show the event now under "My Registered Events."

**Say:**
> "Tap into any event to see the details and register. If your club needs
> extra info — like team size — it'll open their form right after. You'll
> get a reminder the day before, and if the venue changes last-minute,
> you'll know."

---

## Shot 5 — QR check-in at the event (2:05–2:35)

**Show:** On the day of the event, open the Scan page. Either point the
phone's camera at the coordinator's QR code, or tap "Scan with camera"
in-app. Show the success screen ("Checked in!" + points awarded).

**Say:**
> "At the event itself, scan the QR code the coordinator's showing — either
> with your regular camera app, or right inside Synergy. That's it, you're
> checked in and your points land immediately."

---

## Shot 6 — Live Round quiz — coming soon, optional (2:35–3:15)

*Not part of the first event — this is a preview of a feature you'll use at
a future quiz night, not something to expect right away. Fine to cut this
shot entirely for now and re-add it once a real Live Round is actually
scheduled.*

**Show:** From the home screen (or a link/QR the host shares), join a Live
Round with a room code. Show team formation ("Go solo" or joining a
teammate), the question screen (2×2 answer grid), the "Locked in, waiting
for the host…" suspense state, then the reveal (green for correct, orange
for wrong) and the final scoreboard.

**Say:**
> "Down the line, some events will include Live Round — a Kahoot-style
> quiz. Your host gives you a room code. Join solo or team up with
> classmates — if you're on a team, only the team captain answers for
> everyone, so lock in together. Answer, then wait — you won't know if
> you're right until the host reveals it to the whole room. Nothing to do
> with this yet — just so it's not a surprise later."

---

## Shot 7 — Profile & Leaderboard (3:15–3:45)

**Show:** Profile page — stats, activity breakdown (events attended, quiz
participation), streak chain again. Then the Leaderboard — scroll to find
your own name/rank.

**Say:**
> "Your Profile has your full activity history. And the Leaderboard ranks
> every student in the program — everyone shows up, even at zero points, so
> there's always something to climb toward."

---

## Shot 8 — Notifications (3:45–4:00, optional if time allows)

**Show:** Enable notifications from Profile (if not already on). Show a
real notification arriving (a new event, or an update on a registered
event) on the lock screen or notification shade.

**Say:**
> "Turn on notifications and you'll hear about new events the moment
> they're posted — and if anything changes about an event you've already
> registered for."

---

## Closing card (text overlay, no narration needed)

> **Synergy** — Progress, Together.
> One app. Every club. Every point counts.

---

## Notes for whoever edits this together

- Keep each shot's screen recording, trim dead air, add the "Say" lines as
  either voiceover (recorded separately, cleaner) or on-screen captions —
  captions are safer if multiple people might record different shots.
- Shot 6 (Live Round) is the first one to cut if the video needs trimming
  for length or timing — it's the only shot that isn't about this first
  event. Shots 1–5 and 7 (install through leaderboard) are the ones that
  actually matter for the students walking into this event.
- If a shot didn't go cleanly live (e.g., no real event was open for
  attendance that day), it's fine to stage it once with a real disposable
  test event — just don't present it as "live" if it wasn't.
